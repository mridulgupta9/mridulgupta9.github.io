I am a Computer Vision Algorithm Engineer at KLA working on detecting defects in photomasks.

My research focuses on applying deep learning and machine learning tools to problems in computer vision and image processing.

I earned my Ph.D. from the Video and Image Processing Laboratory (VIPER) at Purdue University advised by Prof. Edward J. Delp.

I also earned my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Purdue University.

I received a Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering from Indian Institue of Technology (IIT) Roorkee, India where I worked in Intelligent Transportation Systems.
